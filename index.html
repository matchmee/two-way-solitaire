Two‑Way Solitaire

Tap a top card: it will auto-move to the best spot (foundations first), or select if no good move.

How to Play (Two‑Way / “Flip‑Style”)

  • Goal: move all cards to the four Foundations (A→K). Suit is ignored in this version.
  • On Tableaus you may build up or down. Direction can change anytime; single-card moves only.
  • Seven columns. Cards are fanned so you can see what’s beneath the top.
  • Tap a top card to auto-move it to the best destination (Foundations preferred; otherwise a legal Tableau). If nothing fits, it will just select.
  • Tap the Stock to deal one to Waste. When Stock is empty, tap it to recycle Waste (unlimited redeals).
  • Offline capable once installed to Home Screen.
