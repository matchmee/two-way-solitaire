Two‑Way Solitaire

Tap a card, then tap a destination. Build up or down; change direction anytime.

How to Play (Two‑Way / “Flip‑Style” Klondike)

  • Goal: move all cards to the four Foundations (A→K by suit).
  • On the five Tableaus you may build up or down (e.g., 7 on 6 or on 8). Direction can change any time.
  • Suits don’t matter on the Tableaus. Empty Tableau spaces accept any single card.
  • Move one card at a time. (No multi‑card stacks.)
  • Tap the Stock to deal one card to the Waste. Unlimited redeals (when Stock is empty, tap the empty Stock to recycle Waste).
  • Foundations are by suit only (A→K). You can tap‑move eligible cards onto a Foundation.
  • Offline capable: once loaded, you can play without internet.
