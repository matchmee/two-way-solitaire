Two‑Way Solitaire

Stacks auto‑fit to your screen. No more scrolling.

How to Play (Two‑Way / “Flip‑Style”)

  • Goal: move all cards to the four Foundations (A→K). Suit ignored.
  • Tableau: build up or down by 1; single‑card moves only.
  • Seven columns with fanned stacks and face‑down cards that flip when revealed.
  • Tap a top face‑up card to auto‑move (Foundations preferred). If no legal auto move, it selects.
  • Tap Stock to deal one to Waste; tap empty Stock to recycle Waste.
